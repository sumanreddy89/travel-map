List the places you went and add your photos. An animated map traces the route and cuts to your pictures at each stop. TravelMap is free and open source, but running it yourself means Node, a map download and API keys. The hosted version would do that part for you.

The demo film, "Porto to Palma" (50 seconds): by car, train and plane, made entirely by TravelMap.

What you'd get

  • The route draws itself, by car, train or plane.

  • Then your photos at each stop.

  • The whole trip at the end.

How it would work

  1. Paste your trip: the places, in order, with dates if you like.
  2. Add photos or short clips for each stop.
  3. Get an email with a link to your MP4, landscape or portrait.

Prefer to run it yourself? It's free and always will be: github.com/sumanreddy89/travel-map.
