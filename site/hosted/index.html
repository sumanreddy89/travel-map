List the cities you went to this year, with plane, train or car for each leg. You get a film that counts up your countries, cities, kilometres and flights, flies your whole year on a map, and ends on a card you can share. TravelMap is free and open source, but running it yourself means Node and a large map download. The hosted version would do that part for you.

A sample year (48 seconds, with music): seven cities, from Lisbon to Prague, made entirely by TravelMap.

What you'd get

  • Your year, counted up.

  • Every leg flown, by plane, train or car.

  • The whole year at the end, then a card to share.

How it would work

  1. Paste your cities, in order, with dates and plane, train or car if you like.
  2. Pick portrait for your phone or landscape for everything else.
  3. Get an email with a link to your MP4. Photos at each stop are optional, for single trips.

Prefer to run it yourself? It's free and always will be: github.com/sumanreddy89/travel-map.
