# Hosted render: fake-door test

**Question:** will anyone pay to skip the setup?

A landing page (`site/hosted/`, published to GitHub Pages at https://sumanreddy89.github.io/travel-map/hosted/) offers a hosted version that doesn't exist yet: "3 free, then about €3 per film". It says plainly that it isn't live and nothing is charged. Nothing is built behind it until the gate below passes.

## The gate (written before posting, 25 Nov 2026)

Counted over **3 weeks from the first post** (the Reel), across the posts in `docs/launch-notes.md`, in that order:

- **Pass:** 10 or more people ask for a film, by joining the waitlist or clicking "I'd pay for this" (either button counts; a person counts once).
- **Fail:** fewer than 3. TravelMap stays a free portfolio repo; nothing more is built.
- **In between (3 to 9):** treat as a fail for building, but note what people said they'd render.

Revised 26 Nov, before any post: the wording now matches `launch-notes.md` and the test runs across the posts listed there; the thresholds are unchanged.

A person counts once: someone who does both buttons, or the same email twice, is one. My own test visits and submissions don't count (submit one test entry each, then delete it in Tally).

Out of scope until a pass: payments, accounts, cloud rendering, Google Timeline import, GPX.

## Setup (once, before posting)

1. **Tally form "TravelMap waitlist"**: one email field (required), and a line: "Used only to tell you if the hosted version launches. Reply to any email to be removed." Thank-you screen: "You're on the list."
2. **Tally form "TravelMap: I'd pay for this"**: first block "Thanks, that's counted." Then one optional question, "What would you render?" (long text), an optional email field ("if you'd like to hear when it launches"), and a submit button labelled "Count me in".
3. Paste both form links into `site/hosted/config.js` and merge. Until then the buttons show as unavailable.
4. Open the page on a phone and a laptop, click both buttons, submit a test entry, then delete the test entries in Tally.

Results are read in one place: Tally's submissions for the two forms (plus form views, if your Tally plan shows them).
